Workout settings

Allowed punches

With defense off, the coach calls only punches (plus footwork if enabled). Classic mode uses familiar boxing combinations filtered by your selected punches. If your selection has no suitable classics, the coach generates compatible sequences instead.

The pre-round countdown runs before the first round only. During every rest, the coach automatically calls out 3, 2, 1 before the next round. Changes apply when you reset or start a new workout. Settings save on this device.

How it works

During rounds, keep moving between calls. The coach picks familiar combinations, generates fresh ones, or mixes both, while respecting your allowed punches. Callout speed depends on your device’s installed speech voice. At the bell, recover before the next round. To stop, press Pause; to start over, Reset.

Punches: 1 jab · 2 cross · 3 lead hook · 4 rear hook · 5 lead uppercut · 6 rear uppercut
